Windows 10 / 11

  1. Run the installer.
  2. If SmartScreen warns you, choose More info → Run anyway — the beta isn't code-signed yet.
  3. Open WhiteInk from the Start menu.

Android 7.0+

  1. Open the downloaded APK.
  2. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when asked.
  3. Tap Install, then open WhiteInk.

Only download WhiteInk from this page. Beta builds aren't in the Play Store or Microsoft Store yet, so your device may show an "unknown publisher" notice — that's expected.

Questions

Good to know.

Is WhiteInk free?

Yes — the beta is completely free. If pricing ever arrives, you'll hear about it well before the beta ends.

Where is my writing stored?

On your own device. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on sync, which saves a private snapshot of your work to your own cloud storage, tied to your Google sign-in.

Do I need an account?

No. WhiteInk works fully offline with no account. Signing in with Google is only for syncing between devices.

Why does my device show a warning?

The beta isn't signed with a paid publisher certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen and Android show a generic warning for apps installed outside a store. The install steps above walk you through it.

How do I update?

Download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your projects stay where they are.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Open an issue on our GitHub page with what happened and which device you use. Every report shapes the release.
