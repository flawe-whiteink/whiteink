Account

Delete your account

WhiteInk · by Flawe (Praveen Chakkadi)

You can ask us to delete your WhiteInk account, or just your Cloud Sync data, at any time. Deletion is free and doesn’t require the app to be installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email flawestudios@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in to WhiteInk with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my WhiteInk account”, or “Delete my WhiteInk sync data” if you want to keep your account.
  3. We’ll confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Account record: your email address, display name, sign-up date, last sign-in time, and sign-in count.
  • Cloud Sync data: any snapshot of your writing stored in your private sync container. Snapshots already expire on their own after 24 hours.

What we keep

  • Usage event logs (event type, time, and an account ID, with no email or writing). These are kept for service statistics and abuse prevention and are no longer linked to your email once your account is deleted.
  • Audit log of the deletion itself, which stores only a masked email address, as a security record.
  • Support emails, including your deletion request, for up to 24 months.

Writing on your devices

WhiteInk stores your writing locally, so we can’t delete it from your devices for you. To remove it, uninstall WhiteInk or clear its app data on each device. Export anything you want to keep first.
